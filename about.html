Hey, I’m Ajeet.

I’m a product designer who likes making sense of messy problems and turning ideas into things people can actually use. But work is only one part of who I am.

Product designer, Bangalore · four-plus years, B2B and B2C · currently at Porter, before that Juspay and Convin.ai

It probably starts with curiosity.

I’ve always been curious about the world and the things that make it interesting. I like discovering new places, getting lost in unfamiliar streets, picking up a camera and noticing the little things most people walk past, or getting completely absorbed in a good anime and its world of characters and stories.

I like travelling without having every moment planned, leaving enough room for a wrong turn, an unexpected conversation, or a place I wasn’t looking for.

I think I enjoy the feeling of discovering something more than I enjoy knowing exactly what I’m going to find.

Maybe that’s also why I like doing things that pull me into the moment.

Swimming

gives me a rare kind of quiet, where there’s nothing to focus on except breathing, rhythm, and movement.

Fitness

gives me something completely different—it reminds me that progress usually comes from showing up consistently, even when you don’t feel like it.

Somewhere between the two, I’ve learned to appreciate both slowing down and pushing forward. I think that balance has become an important part of who I am.

And that curiosity eventually found its way into what I do.

I like understanding how things work, questioning why they work that way, and turning ideas into something tangible.

Design became one of the ways I could explore that instinct, and now AI has made the space between having an idea and actually building it smaller than ever. I’m constantly experimenting, learning, making things, breaking them, and trying again.

I don’t really see myself as just a designer, photographer, traveller, fitness enthusiast, anime nerd, or builder—they’re all different pieces of the same person.

And I don’t think I want to have everything figured out either. I’d rather keep exploring, keep learning, and see where all these interests take me next.

That’s me. Now let’s talk about you.

Drop a ‘hi’ and let’s start cooking something together.
